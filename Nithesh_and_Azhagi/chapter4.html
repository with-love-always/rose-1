The Moon Isn't Beautiful

DECEMBER NIGHTS ❄️

It was cold that night.

I told you something strange.

“The moon isn’t beautiful,” I said.

You asked me why.


“Because it’s a thief of light.

It has no fire of its own.

It only shines because it borrows from the sun.”

But you...

You don't need the sun.

You glow on your own, my Azhagi.

You are the light.

That was my first Valentine.

My first real moment of courage.

So tell me...

Can we continue this?

Not just today.

But always?
