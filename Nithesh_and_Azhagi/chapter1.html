The Day You Walked In

You walked into the hotel on your birthday...

with your brother beside you.


But to me,

you didn’t just walk in —

You arrived.

Like an angel who lost her way to heaven.

We ate. We laughed.

We talked like it was casual.

Then your brother went to pay the bill.


Just one small moment.

One Spiderman filter photo. 🕷️❤️

One spark.
