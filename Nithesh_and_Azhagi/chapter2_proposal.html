The Proposal

✨ Tap the moon ✨

The night was quiet.

The moon was bright.

And I told you something strange...

"The moon isn't beautiful," I said.

You asked me why.


"Because it's a thief of light.

It has no fire of its own.

It only shines because it borrows from the sun."

But you...

You don't need the sun.

You glow on your own, my Azhagi.

You are the light.

"Will you be mine?"

You said yes.

That was the moment everything changed.

"Some moments change everything."
